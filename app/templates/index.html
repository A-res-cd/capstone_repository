What CapRE does

Built around one departmental archive

01

Secure Archive

Every finished BSIT capstone lives here with full metadata — title, authors, adviser, specialization, keywords, and the PDF itself, indexed and searchable.

02

Smart Discovery

Filter by program, specialization, or year. Generate an APA 7 citation in one click.

03

Access Requests

Request full manuscript access and track approval status end to end.

04

Analytics for the department

Admins see capstone distribution by specialization, the request pipeline, and which manuscripts get cited most — no separate spreadsheet needed.
