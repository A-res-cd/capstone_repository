What CapRE does

Everything your department needs

Secure Archive

Upload and manage capstone manuscripts with full metadata — title, authors, adviser, program, keywords, and PDF access in one place.

Smart Discovery

Browse and filter capstone projects by program, specialization, year, or keyword. Generate APA 7 citations in one click.

Access Requests

Students request full manuscript access, track approval status, and view approved documents — all through a structured workflow.

Analytics

Admins see capstone distribution by program, request pipeline status, and the most-cited works — all at a glance.
